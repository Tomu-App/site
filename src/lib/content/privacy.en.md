## What we store

When you sign up for news about Tomu, we store:

- your email address,
- the time of your sign-up and the time you confirmed it,
- the version of the consent text you agreed to,
- the language of the page you signed up on (German or English), so we can write to you in that language.

We do not store IP addresses, we do not set cookies, and we do not use analytics or tracking tools.

## What we use the data for

Only to send you occasional emails about Tomu's progress. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

## Double opt-in

After you sign up, we send you an email with a confirmation link. You are only signed up once you confirm it. The time of confirmation and the version of the consent text serve as proof of your consent.

## Retention

- We automatically delete sign-ups that have not been confirmed after 7 days.
- If you unsubscribe, we delete your data immediately. Every email contains an unsubscribe link.

## Service providers

This site runs on Cloudflare as a processor. We use:

- **Cloudflare D1** to store sign-ups, with data stored in the EU,
- **Cloudflare Email Sending** to send the emails,
- **Cloudflare Turnstile**, a check against automated sign-ups. For this, your browser loads a script from challenges.cloudflare.com. Technical data (for example your IP address) may be transmitted to Cloudflare in the process.

We have a data processing agreement with Cloudflare.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object. You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future, for example via the unsubscribe link in every email. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
